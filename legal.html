Section 02

Privacy Policy

This section explains how Buildmate intends to handle information submitted through this website. Review and adjust it to match the actual hosting, analytics, contact, and business practices before launch.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, company, and project details. This static website does not currently include a built-in contact form or payment checkout.

How information is used

Information you send may be used to respond to enquiries, prepare estimates, deliver requested services, communicate about projects, and maintain basic business records.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by email, hosting, or other providers needed to operate the website and respond to you, subject to their own terms and applicable law. We may disclose information where legally required.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry and project information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described or to meet legal and accounting obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information but cannot guarantee absolute security for internet transmissions.

Your choices and requests

You may email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have sent, subject to applicable legal obligations. Depending on your location, additional privacy rights may apply.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not intentionally set analytics cookies through its own code. Hosting providers, embedded third-party resources, or future analytics tools may process technical data; verify the actual configuration and update this notice if it changes.

Children and policy updates

This website is intended for business enquiries and is not directed at children. We may update this policy as the site and services evolve, and will revise the effective date when appropriate.
