Our story

A group of friends.
A shared way of working.

Buildmate is being built by a small team of friends who enjoy making digital things work better. We bring different strengths to the table—development, design, and testing—and work together to deliver thoughtful, dependable results.

We believe good work is more than getting a page online. It means understanding the brief, communicating honestly, checking the details, fixing what breaks, and being clear about what happens next.

We're growing one project at a time, with the goal of becoming a long-term digital partner for the teams we work with.

How we work

A simple, transparent process.

Small projects work best when everyone knows what happens next.

01

Understand

We discuss your goals, users, requirements, and constraints.

02

Plan

We agree on scope, timeline, deliverables, and acceptance criteria.

03

Build

We design and implement the agreed solution with regular updates.

04

Test & refine

We check key flows, responsive layouts, and issues before handover.

05

Launch & support

We support handover and agree on any follow-up maintenance.

What you can expect

A partner who cares about the details.

We’re building our reputation through the way we work—not inflated claims.

01 / Communication

Clear updates, early

02 / Quality

Build, then check

Testing is part of delivery, not an afterthought. We aim to catch issues before handover.

03 / Partnership

Help beyond launch

We can agree on the next fixes and improvements, so support has a clear scope and owner.
